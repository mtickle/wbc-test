New Here?

Welcome! We are so glad you found us. Whether you are exploring faith for the first time or looking for a new church home, there is a place for you here. Come exactly as you are.

We have dedicated, secure environments for infants through 5th grade during all Sunday services. Our background-checked volunteers are ready to help you at the Kids Check-In desk as soon as you arrive.

We are easily accessible from Raleigh, Wendell, Clayton, or Zebulon. When you pull into the lot, look for the reserved First-Time Guest parking right near the main entrance.

Let our welcome team know that you are a first-time guest. We have a free gift for you and a team ready to answer any questions, show you around, or just say hello!

About Our Community

We are a vibrant and welcoming community dedicated to loving God and serving our neighbors. Our mission is to provide a safe and nurturing environment where individuals and families can grow spiritually while building lasting relationships.

Founded on the principles of grace and truth, we believe in the power of community to transform lives. Join us as we journey together in faith, hope, and love. Everyone has a story, and we would love to be a part of yours.
