Wednesday Night Adult Study

The Gospel of Matthew

Join Pastor Adam for an in-depth verse by verse journey through the life and teachings of Jesus Christ.

Study Details

  • When Wednesdays at 6:30 PM
  • Where Large Fellowship Hall
  • Teacher Dr. Adam Smith

The Gospel of Matthew serves as a beautiful bridge between the Old and New Testaments. In this study, we will explore how Matthew presents Jesus as the promised Messiah and the fulfillment of God's redemptive plan.

There is no preparation required, and you do not need to be a biblical scholar to attend. Bring your Bible, a notebook, and an open heart as we learn together in a relaxed and welcoming environment.
