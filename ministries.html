Our Ministries

Faith in action. Discover the many ways we are serving our community, supporting one another, and sharing God's love.

Food Pantry

No one in our community should go hungry. Our food pantry provides fresh groceries, non-perishables, and household essentials to families in need every Saturday morning.

Volunteer or Donate

Ramp Building

We partner with local organizations to design and construct free, safe wheelchair ramps for the elderly and disabled in our community, restoring their freedom and mobility.

Join the Build Team

Veterans Ministry

A dedicated community of veterans supporting veterans. We provide a space for fellowship, prayer, PTSD resources, and practical assistance for those who have served our country.

Connect With Us

Ministerio Hispano

Buscando a Dios juntos. We offer Spanish-language worship services, translated materials, bilingual Bible studies, and dedicated community support for our Spanish-speaking brothers and sisters.

Aprende Más

Homebound & Shut-Ins

Bringing the church to those who cannot attend. Our visitation team provides regular home and hospital visits, delivers meals, serves communion, and prays with our homebound members.

Request a Visit

Prison Ministry

Sharing the hope, forgiveness, and restorative grace of the Gospel with incarcerated men and women. We lead Bible studies inside local facilities and support families affected by incarceration.
