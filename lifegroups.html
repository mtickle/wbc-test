Life Groups

Big churches can feel overwhelming. Life Groups are where we shrink the room, build lasting friendships, and dive deep into scripture together.

The Explorers

Kids (K - 5th)

Journeying through the Bible with fun activities, interactive crafts, and foundational lessons about God's love. We make learning scripture an exciting adventure!

Room: Room 101

The Vanguard

Youth (6th - 12th)

Equipping the next generation to lead with faith. We tackle tough questions, build authentic friendships, and learn how to apply the Gospel to real teenage life.

Room: The Loft

The Trailblazers

Young Adults College / 20s

Navigating the transitions of early adulthood, new careers, and relationships while keeping our roots planted firmly in scripture.

Room: Room 204

The Navigators

Adults (30s - 50s) Married & Single

Finding balance in the busy seasons of parenting, careers, and life through deep biblical study, shared wisdom, and mutual encouragement.

Room: Fellowship Hall B

The Summits

Seniors (60+)

A rich community of legacy builders sharing decades of faith. Our time is focused on faithful prayer, deep theological study, and caring for the broader church family. We have seen God's faithfulness through it all and love to study His word together.

Room: The Parlor
