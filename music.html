Music Ministry

Making a joyful noise and leading our congregation in authentic, heartfelt worship every single week.

Praise Team

Our Praise Team leads the congregation in modern, contemporary worship. Featuring a full band and dedicated vocalists, this group focuses on upbeat praise and deep, reflective worship songs that connect with all generations.

Rehearsal

Wednesdays at 8:30 PM in the Sanctuary

Sanctuary Choir

The Sanctuary Choir honors the rich history of church music by blending beautiful four-part harmonies, classic hymns, and powerful choral arrangements. It is a wonderful community of believers who love to sing together.

Rehearsal

Wednesdays at 7:30 PM in the Sanctuary

Come Sing With Us!
