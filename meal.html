Wednesday Family Meal

Join us around the table for great food and wonderful fellowship before our evening activities begin.

Dinner is Served

We believe some of the best conversations and deepest connections happen over a shared meal. Every Wednesday night, our dedicated kitchen team prepares a delicious, home-cooked dinner for the entire church family.

Whether you are a long-time member or a first-time guest, there is a seat saved just for you. Come straight from work or school, relax, and let us take care of the cooking.

  • When Wednesdays at 5:30 PM
  • Where Large Fellowship Hall
  • Cost Suggested donation of $5 per person, or $15 max per family. First-time guests eat free!
